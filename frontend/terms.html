2 Platform Role & Zero-Middleman Architecture

KhetSe operates as an advanced peer-to-peer agricultural marketplace and direct digital connection platform. Our platform enables farmers, grower collectives (FPOs), and agricultural producers to interact and trade directly with bulk commercial buyers, institutional procurers, retailers, and consumers without traditional middlemen, intermediaries, or speculative cartels.

Zero Commission Charter: KhetSe does not charge percentage broker cuts or commission markups on harvest sale prices. All prices displayed are direct farmgate or negotiated wholesale prices.

3 Seller & Farmer Commitments

When creating a crop listing, sellers agree to the following standards:

  • Accurate Quality Grading: All descriptions regarding crop variety, grade (Grade A, B, or Organic), moisture percentage, and harvest age must be genuine and verifiable.
  • Harvest Readiness: The listed lot must be prepared, bagged or crated according to agreed packaging specifications, and ready for pickup at the declared farm location.
  • Compliance with Safety Standards: Produce must adhere to statutory pesticide residue limits, food safety regulations, and cleanliness norms.

4 Buyer Sourcing & Escrow Payment

Commercial buyers and retailers agree to conduct transactions with integrity:

  • Escrow Security: Upon confirming a purchase order, the total order amount is placed into a secured escrow account held by a regulated banking partner.
  • Inspection Window: The buyer has a 24-hour inspection window from delivery to inspect the produce and verify that it matches lot specifications.
  • Automatic Release: If no dispute is filed within 24 hours of documented physical delivery, the escrow funds are released automatically to the seller.

5 Logistics & Cold-Chain Standards

Logistics coordination arranged through KhetSe utilizes verified freight carriers equipped with tracking telematics:

  • Perishable produce is transported via temperature-monitored refrigerated or ventilated vehicles.
  • Transit times and handover milestones are digitally timestamped at pickup and delivery locations.
  • Carrier transit insurance covers vehicular accidents, severe delays exceeding agreed tolerance, and mechanical cooling failure during transit.

6 Quality Inspection, Rejections & Dispute Protocol

In the event of a discrepancy in delivered produce:

  • Immediate Notification: The buyer must flag the issue within the 24-hour inspection window with clear photographic or video evidence through the order dashboard.
  • Objective Evaluation: KhetSe's quality inspection team or designated regional field specialists review the submission against origin quality certificates.
  • Resolution Paths: Verified substandard produce qualifies for (a) proportional discount settlement mutually approved by both parties, (b) return of rejected portions with immediate escrow refund, or (c) replacement from an adjacent verified supplier lot.

7 Guaranteed 48-Hour Settlements

KhetSe is committed to fast farmer liquidity. Funds released from escrow are processed via direct NEFT/RTGS or UPI to the farmer's registered bank account within a maximum of 48 hours of lot acceptance.

Sellers incur zero transaction deductions, factoring fees, or delayed payment penalty charges.

8 Prohibited Conduct & Account Suspension

Users must not engage in harmful marketplace practices, including:

  • Attempting to collude, manipulate mandi price benchmarks, or fix purchase bids artificially.
  • Misrepresenting produce origins, chemical residues, or forged organic certificates.
  • Circumventing platform safety protections to execute off-platform transactions after establishing contact via KhetSe.
  • Harassing, threatening, or engaging in abusive behavior toward farmers, buyers, or logistics staff.

Violation may result in immediate permanent account termination and forfeiture of marketplace privileges.

9 Disclaimers & Limitation of Liability

While KhetSe rigorously verifies member credentials and standardizes logistics, agricultural commodities are inherently subject to natural weather conditions, seasonal shelf-life variances, and force majeure events. KhetSe shall not be held liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from unpreventable climatic transit delays.
