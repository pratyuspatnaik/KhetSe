Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Have a quick question? The answers to the most common queries are right below.

How fast do farmers receive payouts after delivering crops?
KhetSe guarantees payouts within 48 hours of lot inspection at pickup or delivery. Funds are deposited directly into the farmer's verified bank account or UPI without any middleman cuts or hidden commission deductions.
How does KhetSe verify produce quality and freshness?
Every listing is cataloged with standardized grading metrics (moisture content, size grading, pesticide residue clearance, and harvest date). Our regional quality specialists perform random sampling checks at farmgate handover before cold-chain dispatch.
What happens if a buyer receives substandard or damaged produce?
Buyers have a 24-hour inspection window upon delivery. If the consignment deviates from the agreed lot specifications, our digital grievance portal enables immediate photo submission, dispute mediation, and automatic escrow hold or replacement.
Can buyers request customized recurring delivery schedules?
Yes! Supermarkets, restaurant chains, and institutional procurement teams can set up weekly recurring farm contracts with fixed delivery windows through our dedicated B2B procurement desk.
How can cold-chain and freight operators partner with KhetSe?
Transport providers with reefer trucks or farm pickup vehicles can apply via the form above (selecting "Logistics Partner"). Our transport onboarding team verifies vehicle telematics, GPS, and cold storage calibration within 48 hours.
