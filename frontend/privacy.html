Our Privacy Commitments at a Glance

No Third-Party Ad Brokers: We never monetize or sell your harvest or pricing data.
Direct Payout Security: Bank account and UPI data are encrypted with AES-256 standard.
Purpose-Limited Traceability: Location coordinates are used only for order delivery and pickup.
Full Data Control: You can request data export or account record deletion at any time.

1 Scope & Applicability

This Privacy Policy applies to all services, websites, mobile interfaces, and digital APIs operated by KhetSe ("we", "us", or "our"). By registering as a Farmer, Seller, Buyer, Institutional Purchaser, or Delivery Partner, you acknowledge and consent to the data collection and handling procedures described herein.

Our core mission is to remove intermediary exploitation in agricultural trade. To fulfill this, we collect only the minimum required information necessary to execute direct trade transactions, enforce quality standards, ensure cold-chain logistics, and disburse prompt 48-hour payments.

2 Information We Collect

We collect several categories of personal and commercial data to facilitate agricultural commerce:

  • Identity and KYC Data: Farmer name, government ID (such as Aadhaar or voter ID for identity verification), farm registration numbers, business PAN, and GSTIN (for wholesale corporate buyers).
  • Contact Information: Active mobile telephone numbers, verified email addresses, physical farm addresses, village revenue blocks, and delivery warehouse addresses.
  • Agricultural & Listing Data: Crop varieties grown, estimated harvest yields, packaging formats, certifications (e.g., Organic, GAP), and historical lot quality inspection reports.
  • Financial & Payout Data: Bank account numbers, IFSC codes, UPI handles, and settlement transaction logs required to credit sales proceeds within 48 hours.
  • Device & Interaction Data: IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, and platform navigation telemetry used to prevent duplicate fake bids and fraud.

3 How We Use Your Data

KhetSe uses collected data solely for authentic business purposes related to agricultural transactions:

  • Enabling seamless direct discovery between producers and commercial buyers without middleman commissions.
  • Facilitating instant trade contracts, invoicing, electronic delivery waybills, and digital receipts.
  • Processing fast financial settlements directly to farmers upon buyer inspection and acceptance.
  • Routing agricultural freight vehicles, calculating optimal pickup routes, and coordinating temperature-controlled transit.
  • Maintaining high marketplace integrity through automated fraud detection, dispute arbitration, and rating systems.
Zero Middleman Principle: We do not disclose farmer supply prices to unauthorized speculative buyers. Prices and listings are visible exclusively to registered, verified purchasers.

4 Sharing & Disclosure of Information

We strictly limit third-party disclosures to entities indispensable to fulfilling your transactions:

  • Counterparties: When an order is placed, the buyer receives the seller's farm location and produce lot information; the seller receives the buyer's fulfillment contact and destination warehouse details.
  • Certified Logistics Carriers: Truck drivers and fleet operators receive only the pickup and destination geolocations, contact numbers, and bill of lading data.
  • Payment Gateways & Banking Partners: Regulated banking institutions and NPCI gateways receive payout details to process electronic transfers.
  • Regulatory Authorities: We may share records if mandated by statutory law, court subpoenas, or agricultural tax compliance orders.

5 Geolocation & Traceability

KhetSe provides full farm-to-fork batch traceability. When a harvest lot is listed, GPS coordinates or village landmarks are stored to facilitate carrier dispatch and buyer lot verification.

Drivers using our logistics partner app transmit real-time telematics during transit so buyers and sellers can monitor fresh produce ETA. Background location tracking ceases immediately upon delivery sign-off.

6 Security & Encryption

We implement bank-grade technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • All data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols.
  • Sensitive financial credentials and identification documents are stored using AES-256 encrypted databases with restricted role-based access.
  • Periodic automated vulnerability assessments and penetration tests are conducted to maintain platform resilience.

7 Your Rights & Control

You have full sovereignty over your personal information:

  • Access & Rectification: You can review and edit your profile, bank details, and farm listings directly from your account settings.
  • Data Portability: You can download your transaction records, past payout sheets, and invoices in PDF or CSV format.
  • Account Deletion: You can request closure of your account and purge of personal identifiers, subject to statutory taxation record retention mandates (7 years under applicable commercial laws).

8 Cookies & Storage Technologies

KhetSe uses essential session cookies to keep you signed in, remember your cart items, and store interface preferences (such as selected language and mandi region). We do not use third-party behavioral advertising cookies.
